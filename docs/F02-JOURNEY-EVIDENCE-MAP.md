## Evidence rules

- **Evidence present** means the referenced source/test covers the stated behavior, not the entire journey.
- **Partial** means the current repository covers a slice of the journey; missing controls must not be inferred.
- **Missing** means no implementation or adequate test evidence was identified in the reviewed slice.
- A negative test for one boundary does not establish all tenant, scope, field, permission, entitlement or workflow boundaries.
- The map records the current implementation honestly. It does not imply product-owner acceptance of every candidate journey or certify production readiness.
